Our story

A Dubai house for Pakistani dress.

We've dressed women in Dubai since {{ site.founded }}. First in our boutique. Then in private appointments. Then at exhibitions and pop-ups.

Now the whole house is online, and it ships to wherever you are.

Falakposh means dressed in the sky.

فلک (falak) is the sky. پوش (posh) means dressed in. Put together, it's what we try to make: clothes that feel as wide and as light as the sky over Dubai.

So we name every piece for something up there. Shafaq is the red glow after sunset. Mahtab is moonlight. Kehkashan is the Milky Way.

You'll never buy a piece called "Style 204".
