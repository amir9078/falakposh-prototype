How to measure.

  • Bust: round the fullest part, with the tape level under your arms.
  • Waist: round the narrowest part, usually just above the navel.
  • Hip: round the fullest part of your hips, feet together.
  • Shirt length: from the top of the shoulder, down to where you want the hem.
  • Sleeve: from the shoulder point to the wrist, arm slightly bent.
  • Trouser length: from your waist down to the ankle bone.

Between two sizes? Take the bigger one. We can take it in, but we can't add cloth that isn't there.
