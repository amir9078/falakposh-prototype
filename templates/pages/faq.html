Will I pay customs when it arrives? {{ icons['plus']|safe }}

Some countries charge import duty. Where we can, we collect it at checkout so nothing is due at the door.

Cards, Apple Pay and pay-in-4 plans at checkout. Prices show in your currency.

Most take 10 to 12 weeks from the first visit. Book early if your date is close, and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Ready-to-wear and unused unstitched fabric, within 14 days. Pieces stitched for you can't be returned, but we'll fix the fit. See shipping and returns.
