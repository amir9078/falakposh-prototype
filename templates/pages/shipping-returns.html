What it costs.

UAE delivery is free over AED {{ site.free_shipping_uae }}. Everywhere else, you see the exact cost at checkout before you pay.

Some countries charge import duty or tax when a parcel arrives. Where we can, we collect it at checkout so nothing is due at the door. If we can't for your country, checkout tells you.

Returns.

  • Ready-to-wear: return it within 14 days, unworn, with the tags on.
  • Unstitched fabric: return it within 14 days if it's uncut and unused.
  • Stitched for you, and bridal: these are made for you, so we can't take them back. We'll alter the fit for free instead.

To start a return, message us on WhatsApp or email {{ site.email }} with your order number.

Something wrong?

If a piece arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours with a photo. We'll put it right at our cost.
