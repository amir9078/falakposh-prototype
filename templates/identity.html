The name, written the way a calligrapher writes it: the letters from right to left, then the dots, and last the dot of the ف, which is a star. Falak is the sky.

The mark

Calligraphy first, English second

{{ mark() }}
  1. i.
    Real NastaliqThe letters are Gulzar Nastaliq outlines, shaped the way Urdu is written and set as vector art, so they print and scale cleanly.
  2. ii.
    The star is the dotThe dot of the ف is a four-point zari star. The name means dressed in the sky; the star sits where the sky is.
  3. iii.
    English for everyone elseFALAKPOSH in Bodoni caps, spaced wide, for labels, the header and anywhere Urdu would be too small.
